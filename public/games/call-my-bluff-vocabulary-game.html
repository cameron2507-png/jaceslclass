How to play

Read the word aloud and the three definitions. Players vote for the definition they think is true, then click it to check.

  • As a team game, give each definition to a different player. They read it aloud and try to convince the other team it’s true.
  • Ask learners to explain why they believe or doubt each definition. That’s where the speaking happens.
  • Every word is real English. Most are informal or humorous British words.
