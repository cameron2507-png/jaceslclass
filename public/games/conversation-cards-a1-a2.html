Conversation Cards

Thirty cards about work and everyday life.

A1/A2

    Useful words

    Use the arrow keys or Space to move between cards, and S to shuffle.

    How to play

    Read the example sentence together, then ask the questions in order. They start easy and get a little harder.

    • In pairs, one person asks and the other answers. Swap at the next card.
    • Try to use at least two of the useful words in your answers.
    • Choose Work or Everyday to practise one area at a time.
