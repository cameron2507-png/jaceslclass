How to play

Split the class into two teams. Team 1 tries to connect a path from top to bottom. Team 2 tries to connect left to right. Teams take turns choosing a hexagon — answer the question correctly to claim it. The first team to complete an unbroken path across the board wins. Click a team name above to switch turns manually.
