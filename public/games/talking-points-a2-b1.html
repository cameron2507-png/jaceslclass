Talking Points

Pick a card, speak for thirty seconds, then answer the follow-ups.

A2/B1

    0:30
    Speak for

    Choose at least one topic to see a card.

    Press Space for the next card and T to start or pause the timer.

    How to play

    One person takes a card and speaks about it until the timer runs out. Then the group asks the follow-up questions.

    • Tap the topics at the top to remove them from the deck.
    • Thirty seconds is a good start at this level. Move up to 45 or 60 when the group is ready.
    • Cards don’t repeat until the whole deck has been used.
