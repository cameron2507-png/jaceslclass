The Map in the Bookshop

A choose-your-own-adventure story in the past simple.

A2/B1
How to use it

Read each part of the story and choose what Emma does next. There are four different endings.

  • Every past simple verb is underlined. Hover or tap it to see the base form.
  • At the end, you get a list of all the verbs on your path.
  • In class, let the group vote on each choice, then retell the story in their own words.
