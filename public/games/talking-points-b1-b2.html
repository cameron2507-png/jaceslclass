Talking Points

Pick a card, give your opinion, then defend it.

B1/B2

Argue the opposite of what you really think.

    0:30
    Speak for

    Choose at least one topic to see a card.

    Press Space for the next card and T to start or pause the timer.

    How to play

    One person takes a card and gives their opinion until the timer runs out. The others then use the follow-up questions to push back.

    • Tap Essential oils and Biomass to remove the industry cards, so the deck works for any group.
    • Turn on Devil’s advocate for a harder round: the speaker must argue the opposite of what they really think.
    • Cards don’t repeat until the whole deck has been used.
