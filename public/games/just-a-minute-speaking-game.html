How to play

The speaker talks on the topic for one minute. Other players can challenge if they hear:

  • Hesitation: a long pause, or “erm” and “um”.
  • Repetition: the same important word used twice (small words like “the” don’t count).
  • Deviation: going off the topic.

A correct challenge wins a point, and the challenger takes over the topic for the time left. Whoever is speaking when the minute ends wins a bonus point. Topics don’t repeat until all 30 have been used.
