    Press Start round when the describer is ready. Only the describer should look at the screen.

    How to play

    One player describes the word at the top to their own team, without saying it or any of the banned words. Gestures and sounds are not allowed.

    • Got it: the team guessed the word. One point.
    • Skip: move to the next card. No points.
    • Taboo!: the describer said a banned word. The other team calls it, and the team loses a point.
    • When time is up, the other team takes a turn.
