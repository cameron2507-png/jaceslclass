Past Simple Video Poker

Every card carries a verb. Get its past simple wrong and the card doesn’t count.

A2/B1
Credits 100Hand 1 of 10Verbs right 0/0

Press Deal to start. Each hand costs 5 credits.

How to play and what pays

Press Deal. Tap the cards you want to keep, then press Draw to replace the others. Before the hand is scored, give the past simple of every verb in your hand. A wrong answer breaks that card, so it can’t help your hand.

Ten hands per game. Your stamp at the end depends on how many verbs you got right.
