A B2 business-English Taboo game built around vocabulary that comes up in real meetings: agenda, deadline, brainstorm, stakeholder, KPI, milestone, pivot, and twelve more.

## Why it works for adult learners

Corporate learners often "know" these words passively but struggle to explain them when they can't fall back on a translation or the obvious synonyms. Forcing paraphrase under time pressure is exactly the skill they need in real meetings — and it surfaces the gaps in their active vocabulary.

## Lesson ideas

- **Pre-meeting warm-up** in an in-house corporate course
- **Vocabulary consolidation** at the end of a business-English unit
- **Speaking fluency boost** for learners who default to one-word answers
- **Confidence builder** before a presentation skills lesson

## Variations

- **Whole-class version**: one student at the front, the rest of the class guesses
- **Written variation**: students work in pairs to write their own taboo cards using vocabulary from a recent unit
- **Harder version**: add the rule that learners can't use any French words either
