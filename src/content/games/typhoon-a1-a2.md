## How it works

A 5×5 grid hides points and special squares. Teams take turns choosing a square. Answer the question correctly and you find out what’s underneath. A wrong answer wins nothing.

## Special squares

- **Typhoon**: the other team loses all its points, unless it has a shield
- **Double**: your score is doubled
- **Steal**: take 20 points from the other team
- **Shield**: protects you from the next Typhoon

The squares are shuffled every game, so no two games are the same.

## What’s covered

Opposites, the past simple of common verbs, plurals, days and months, telling the time, prepositions, and everyday work English like answering the phone.
