A beginner-friendly Typhoon game mixing simple workplace English with everyday topics: numbers, times, routines, present simple, past simple and polite phrases.

## How it works

- 2–6 teams on a 6×6 grid
- 54 questions, so a wrong answer swaps in a fresh one
- Hidden points and specials: Typhoon, Swap, Steal, Double, Shield, Bomb
- You can edit or replace the questions
