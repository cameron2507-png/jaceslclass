A classroom Typhoon game built around job interview English. Questions cover talking about your experience, strengths and weaknesses, why you want the job, and short speaking tasks like leaving a voicemail.

## How it works

- 2–6 teams with editable names
- 36 questions, each with tips the teacher can reveal
- Hidden points and specials: Typhoon, Swap, Steal, Double, Shield, Bomb
- Undo, manual score changes and automatic saving
