## How it works

Each game picks 15 words at random from a bank of 30 weather words. The French translation is shown as a clue. Learners guess letters on screen or with the keyboard, and six wrong guesses lose the word.

## Vocabulary

*sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, foggy, snow, storm, thunder, lightning, rainbow, hail, frost, ice, heatwave, temperature, forecast, umbrella, shower, breeze, drizzle, humid, freezing, degree, cloud, sunshine, puddle, flood, drought, mild, chilly*

## Classroom tips

- After each word, ask learners to use it in a sentence about today’s weather
- Play in teams, taking turns to guess letters
- Follow up with a weather forecast speaking activity
