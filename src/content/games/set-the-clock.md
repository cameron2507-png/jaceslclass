## How it works

A time appears in words, such as *quarter past three*. Learners move the hands with the plus and minus buttons and press **Check**. A correct answer scores a point. A wrong answer shows the right time on the clock and in digits, so learners can see the difference.

## What's included

- **Three levels**: o'clock and half past, quarters, and all five-minute times
- **Colour-coded hands**: navy for hours, teal for minutes, matching the controls
- **Ten rounds** with a running score and a friendly end screen
- Button controls that work well on tablets and interactive whiteboards

## Variations

- Play as a class on the board, with learners taking turns at the controls
- Say the time aloud instead of reading it, for a listening version
- Ask learners to say the time in words after each correct answer
