A one-player version of Typhoon for learners getting ready for a job interview in English. Every square hides a typical interview question or a short workplace task, such as a voicemail or a polite email. After writing an answer, the learner sees a model answer to compare with.

## How it works

- 36 squares on a 6×6 grid
- Each square hides points or a surprise (Typhoon, Double, Bomb and more)
- Model answers for every question
- Progress saves automatically, so you can come back later
