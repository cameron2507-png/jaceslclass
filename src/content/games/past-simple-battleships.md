## How it works

Four ships are hidden on a 6×6 grid: a Battleship (4 cells), a Cruiser (3), a Destroyer (2), and a Patrol Boat (1). Tap any cell to fire — but first you must answer a past simple question correctly. Get it wrong and you waste your shot.

You have 20 questions total, so accuracy matters. A fleet tracker shows which ships are damaged and which are sunk.

## Grammar categories

- **Regular verbs** — forming the past with *-ed*
- **Irregular verbs** — *go/went*, *buy/bought*, *write/wrote*, *drink/drank*
- **Negatives** — *didn't* + base form
- **Questions** — *Did* + subject + base form
- **Time expressions** — *yesterday*, *last week*, *ago*, *in 2019*

## Features

- 🇫🇷/🇬🇧 **bilingual toggle** — switch all questions and explanations between English and French
- **Fleet damage tracker** — visual display of each ship's status
- **Tiered end-of-game verdicts** — Cadet, Lieutenant, Captain, or Admiral depending on accuracy
- **Answer review** — review every question after the game

## Variations

- For stronger groups, set a time limit per question (use a phone timer)
- For weaker groups, allow discussion in pairs before answering
- Play as a class — project on the board, teams take turns choosing cells
