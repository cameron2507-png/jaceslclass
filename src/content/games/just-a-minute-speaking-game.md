## How it works

Based on the BBC radio game. The speaker draws a topic and tries to talk for sixty seconds without hesitation, repetition or deviation. Other players challenge when they hear a mistake.

## What’s included

- 30 topics: 14 on travel and tourism, 16 general (*coffee*, *being late*, *the future of work*)
- A 60-second countdown ring that turns terracotta in the last ten seconds
- Topics don’t repeat until all 30 have been used
- Press Space to draw a new topic

## Classroom tips

- For B1 groups, start at 30 seconds and relax the repetition rule
- Ask the audience for one follow-up question after each turn
- A great warm-up for fluency and confidence
