This is a classic hangman game with fifteen hotel and travel vocabulary words at A2 level: reception, checkout, breakfast, balcony, concierge, keycard, minibar, and more.

## Vocabulary covered

The words have been chosen to match common A2 textbook units on travel and hospitality. Each word also includes a clue to help weaker students — you can hide the clue to make the game harder.

## Lesson ideas

- **Pre-teach** the lexical set, then play hangman to reinforce
- **Review** at the end of a unit on hotels — students should already know the words
- **Vocabulary check-in** at the start of a new lesson on travel
- **Pair work**: students play in pairs on a tablet or phone, with one acting as the "host"

## Skills

Spelling, reading, alphabet recall, lexical recognition.
