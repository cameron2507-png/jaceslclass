## How it works

A 25-clue Jeopardy board that takes irregular verbs beyond lists and into full sentences. Some answers come with a short note explaining the rule, which is useful for discussion.

## Categories

- **Past simple stories**: two verbs per sentence
- **Present perfect**: *I’ve just had lunch*, *We’ve known each other since school*
- **Negatives and questions**: *didn’t see*, *Did they leave?*, *Has he written…?*
- **Fix the mistake**: *I have went*, *I’ve saw that film last week*
- **Odd one out**: verb patterns like *brought, bought, thought*

## Classroom tips

- Ask teams to explain the rule behind each correction
- Use the Odd one out category to introduce verb groups
- Follow up with a short written task using five verbs from the game
