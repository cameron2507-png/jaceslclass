## How it works

A describer from one team sees the target word and five banned words. They have a set time to get their team to guess as many words as possible. The card stays hidden until the round starts, so only the describer sees it.

## What’s included

- 30 cards on everyday life and work: *deadline, passport, salary, retirement, promotion, volunteer* and more
- Team names and scores
- Round length of 45, 60 or 90 seconds
- Got it, Skip and Taboo! buttons

## Classroom tips

- Before playing, brainstorm ways to describe a word: *It’s a kind of…*, *You use it when…*, *It’s the opposite of…*
- Weaker groups can play with only three banned words. Cover the last two
- Ask learners to write new cards for the next lesson
