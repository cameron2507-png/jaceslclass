## How it works

Teams choose a category and a value from the 25-square board. The teacher reads the clue, reveals the answer and gives the points to the team that got it right.

## Categories

- **Give me the past simple**: *get, give, know, forget, fall*
- **Give me the past participle**: *do, see, write, speak, drive*
- **All three forms**: *take, took, taken* and more
- **Same or different?**: spot verbs like *buy* (bought, bought) and *put* (put, put, put)
- **In a sentence**: past simple and present perfect in context

## Classroom tips

- Ask the team to use the verb in their own sentence for a bonus
- Pair it with an irregular verbs list for revision
- Move on to the A2/B1 board for present perfect practice
