## How it works

A word appears on screen. Both players try to pronounce it. Tap **Reveal Pronunciation** to show the IPA transcription and a short note explaining why the word is tricky. Then award the point.

## Difficulty levels

- **Medium** — silent letters, disappearing syllables, stress traps (*comfortable*, *Wednesday*, *iron*, *receipt*…)
- **Hard** — misleading spellings, consonant clusters, stress shifts (*colonel*, *eighth*, *architecture*, *thoroughly*…)
- **Evil** — pronunciation boss fights (*Worcestershire*, *squirrel*, *synecdoche*, *otorhinolaryngologist*…)
- **Mixed** — pulls from all three levels randomly

## Why these words?

Every word targets a specific sound or pattern that causes problems for French-speaking learners: silent consonants, unexpected vowel reductions, consonant clusters that don't exist in French, and words borrowed from French but pronounced completely differently in English.

## Variations

- Use **Medium** only for A2/B1 groups
- Use **Evil** as a fun warm-up challenge for strong B2/C1 groups
- Set it up as a team game — each team nominates a speaker per round
