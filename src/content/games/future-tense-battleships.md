A solo battleships game where learners answer future tense grammar questions to fire at a hidden fleet on a 6×6 grid. Twenty questions across five categories — will/won't, going to, present continuous for future, first conditional, and timetables — with a bilingual English/French toggle throughout.

## Why it works for adult learners

The future tenses are one of the trickiest areas for French-speaking learners because French uses a single future tense where English has several overlapping forms. This game forces learners to choose between will, going to, and present continuous by reading context clues carefully — exactly the skill they need in real communication. The battleships mechanic keeps motivation high without adding cognitive noise.

## Lesson ideas

- **Pre-teaching consolidation** after introducing will vs going to vs present continuous
- **Revision warm-up** before a unit test on future forms
- **Self-study homework** — learners play solo and screenshot their final score
- **Projected whole-class version** — the teacher clicks, the class votes on the answer

## Variations

- **Team battle**: two teams alternate turns, each answering a question before choosing a cell
- **Speed round**: set a 15-second timer per question for advanced groups
- **Explain your choice**: after answering, the learner must explain why they chose that form — reinforces the grammar rule beyond just pattern-matching
