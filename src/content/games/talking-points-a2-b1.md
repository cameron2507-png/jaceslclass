## How it works

A card shows one speaking prompt. The speaker starts the timer and talks until it runs out, then the rest of the group reveals the follow-up questions and keeps the conversation going.

## What's included

- **48 cards** across six topics: Everyday life, Food and drink, Free time, Travel and places, Work and study, and People and things
- **Two follow-up questions** on every card, hidden until you need them
- **Adjustable timer** of 20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds, with 30 seconds as the default
- **Topic filter**: tap a topic to take it out of the deck
- Cards don't repeat until the whole deck has been used

## Variations

- Start at 30 seconds and move up to 60 as the group grows in confidence
- Play in pairs, with the listener choosing which follow-up to ask
- Use one topic per lesson as a warm-up
