## How it works

It plays like Jacks or Better video poker, with one twist: every card carries a verb. After the draw, players choose the past simple of each verb from four options. A wrong answer breaks that card, so a full house can disappear with one mistake.

## What’s included

- 52 verbs, mostly irregular (*go, bring, catch, choose, forget*) plus regular spelling traps (*stopped, studied, planned*)
- Distractors based on real learner errors (*goed*, *brang*, *catched*)
- A standard pay table, from Jacks or better to Royal flush
- Ten hands per game, with a final stamp based on accuracy

## Classroom tips

- Play in pairs: one person chooses the cards, the other answers the verbs
- Ask learners to make a sentence with any verb they got wrong
- Works well as a homework or self-study game
