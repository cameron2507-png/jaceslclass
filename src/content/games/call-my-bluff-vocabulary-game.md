## How it works

Each word comes with three definitions in random order. One is real, and the other two are bluffs. Learners discuss which they believe, then click to check. The game keeps score over 20 words.

## Words

*kerfuffle, flummox, skedaddle, codswallop, collywobbles, gormless, lollygag, brouhaha, gallivant, bumf, chinwag, mollycoddle, skulduggery, widdershins, kibosh, discombobulated, higgledy-piggledy, snickersnee, gobsmacked, hobnob*

## Classroom tips

- As a team game, give each definition to a different student to read aloud as convincingly as possible
- Ask learners to justify their vote. That’s where the speaking happens
- Challenge groups to write their own bluffs for a new word
