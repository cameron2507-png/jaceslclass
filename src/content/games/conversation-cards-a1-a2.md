## How it works

Each card has a topic, an example sentence to model the language, four questions that build from simple to slightly harder, and a set of useful words.

## What's included

- **15 work cards**: my job, my working day, meetings, emails and calls, business trips, skills and more
- **15 everyday cards**: food, home, family, weekends, travel, weather, sport, shopping and more
- **Deck filter** to practise Work or Everyday on its own
- Back, next and shuffle controls, with keyboard shortcuts

## Variations

- Pairs: one person asks, the other answers, then swap at the next card
- Challenge learners to use two of the useful words in each answer
- Beginners can answer only the first two questions
