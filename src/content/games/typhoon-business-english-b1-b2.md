A Typhoon game for intermediate business English classes, with 36 questions on error correction, tenses, phrasal verbs, false friends like actually/currently, and short speaking tasks.

## How it works

- 2–6 teams with editable names
- Hidden points from 5 to 50, plus Typhoon, Swap, Steal, Double, Shield and Bomb
- Edit or replace the questions, one per line
- Undo, manual score changes and automatic saving
