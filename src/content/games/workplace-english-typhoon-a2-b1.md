A Typhoon game for pre-intermediate adults who use English at work. Questions cover present perfect, answering the phone, much/many, common French-speaker mistakes and short speaking tasks.

## How it works

- 2–6 teams on a 6×6 grid
- A wrong answer swaps in a new question
- Hidden points and specials: Typhoon, Swap, Steal, Double, Shield, Bomb
- Undo, manual score changes and automatic saving
