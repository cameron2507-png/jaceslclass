## How it works

Every clue is an incomplete sentence with four options, in the style of TOEIC Reading Part 5. Teams choose a category and value, pick the correct option, and win the points.

## Categories

- **Prepositions**: *by Friday*, *responsible for*, *increased by*
- **Word forms**: noun, adjective or adverb? (*productivity*, *punctually*)
- **Verb forms**: present perfect, future perfect, conditionals, -ing after *suggest*
- **Connectors**: *however*, *despite*, *provided that*, *whereas*
- **Business vocabulary**: *invoice*, *arrange*, *expand*, *approved*, *target*

## Classroom tips

- Time each answer to match test conditions (about 20 seconds)
- Ask teams to explain why the other three options are wrong
- A good warm-up before a full TOEIC practice test
