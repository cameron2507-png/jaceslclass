## How it works

A 25-clue Jeopardy board built for French speakers learning English. Every clue has a French translation, available as a hint before the answer and shown again with it.

## Categories

- **Give me the past simple**: *go* (aller), *say* (dire), *leave* (partir)…
- **Give me the past participle**: *be* (être), *give* (donner), *forget* (oublier)…
- **What’s the base form?**: *made*, *told*, *paid*, *fought*, *meant*
- **All three forms**: *sleep, slept, slept* and more
- **Same or different?**: spot the patterns in *put*, *sell*, *run*, *send*, *become*

## Classroom tips

- Play first without the French hints, then again with them for weaker groups
- Ask learners to translate the verb back into French as a bonus point
- Ideal for French secondary schools and adult classes in France
