## How it works

Teams choose squares on a 5×5 grid and answer a question for each. A correct answer reveals points or a special square. Typhoon wipes out the other team’s score, so the lead can change at any moment.

## What’s covered

- Present perfect with *for* and *since*
- First conditional (*If it rains, we’ll…*)
- Verb + -ing and verb + to (*enjoy reading*, *decide to take*)
- Comparatives and superlatives
- Workplace vocabulary: *deadline*, *colleague*, *out of order*, ending an email

## Classroom tips

- Ask the team to explain each answer for a bonus
- Squares are shuffled every game, so you can replay it
- Try the B1/B2 version for stronger groups
