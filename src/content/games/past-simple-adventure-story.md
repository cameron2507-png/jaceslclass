## How it works

*The Map in the Bookshop* is a short interactive story. Emma finds a hand-drawn map in an old book, and the reader decides what she does next. There are seven scenes and four different endings.

Every past simple verb is underlined. Tap or hover to see the base form (*went → go*). At the end of each path, a glossary lists every verb the reader met.

## Why it works

- Learners see the past simple in a real story, not in isolated sentences
- Choices give a reason to read carefully and to re-read
- Different paths mean different verbs, so it’s worth reading again

## Classroom tips

- Read it together on the board and vote on each choice
- Ask pairs to retell their path in the past simple
- Challenge stronger learners to write a fifth ending
