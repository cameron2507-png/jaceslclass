## How it works

Players take turns being in the hot seat. When the French player is up, they face English pronunciation nightmares like *squirrel*, *Worcestershire*, and *thoroughly*. When the English player is up, they tackle French horrors like *écureuil*, *serrurerie*, and *grenouille*.

The other player acts as judge. Tap **Reveal Pronunciation** to see the IPA transcription and settle any disputes.

## What's included

### English words (for the French player)
- **Medium** — *comfortable*, *Wednesday*, *iron*, *clothes*…
- **Hard** — *thoroughly*, *colonel*, *queue*, *entrepreneur*…
- **Evil** — *Worcestershire*, *squirrel*, *synecdoche*, *chthonic*…

### French words (for the English player)
- **Medium** — *écureuil*, *grenouille*, *feuille*, *serrurerie*, *oignon*…
- **Hard** — *anticonstitutionnellement*, *accueillir*, *bouilloire*, *quincaillerie*…
- **Evil** — *Auxerre*, *chrysanthème*, *Schaerbeek*…

## Ideal for

Mixed-nationality groups, one-to-one lessons with a French learner (you play the English speaker), or any class where you want to show that pronunciation is hard in *every* language — it levels the playing field and gets plenty of laughs.
