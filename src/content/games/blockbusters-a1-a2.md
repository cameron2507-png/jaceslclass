A classroom take on the iconic British game show *Blockbusters*. Two teams race across a 5×5 hexagonal grid, answering elementary business English questions to claim hexagons and build a connected path.

## What's covered

Twenty-five questions mixing **basic business vocabulary** (office, customer, employee, meeting, warehouse, schedule, invoice) with **elementary grammar** (have/has, present simple, basic phrasal verbs, modals like *need* and *can*).

## How to play

- Split the class into two teams of roughly equal ability.
- Team 1 tries to connect the **top to the bottom** of the board.
- Team 2 tries to connect the **left to the right**.
- Teams take turns choosing any hexagon. Read out the question — if the team answers correctly, they claim that hex in their colour.
- Both teams need a minimum path of 5 connected hexagons to win.
- Tap a team name above the board to switch turns manually if needed.

## Teaching tips

- For very low-level groups, allow students to discuss in their team before answering.
- Encourage full sentences in the answer where the question allows.
- After a hex is claimed, ask the class to use the answer word in a sentence as a quick extension.
- If a question is too easy or too hard for a group, click "No one" to skip and let the next team choose.
