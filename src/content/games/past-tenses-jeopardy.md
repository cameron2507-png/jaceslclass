A four-by-four Jeopardy board for B1 students reviewing the three main past tenses. Includes a "mixed" category with translation, error correction, and irregular verbs.

## Categories

- **Past simple** — affirmative, negative, and question forms
- **Past continuous** — when to use it and how to form it
- **Present perfect** — *for* vs *since*, finished vs unfinished time
- **Mixed** — irregular verbs, error correction, French → English translation

## Lesson ideas

- **End-of-unit review** before a written assessment
- **Friday treat lesson** after a heavy grammar week
- **Cover lesson** if you need an off-the-shelf 30-minute activity that any teacher can run

## How to extend

Print the question list as a worksheet for absent students. After the game, get pairs to write three new questions in the same style — a great way to consolidate.
