A classroom take on the iconic British game show *Blockbusters*. Two teams race across a 5×5 hexagonal grid, answering intermediate business English questions to claim hexagons and build a connected path.

## What's covered

Twenty-five questions mixing **corporate vocabulary** (acquisition, merger, outsourcing, stakeholders, KPIs, USP, venture capital, downsizing) with **advanced grammar structures** (third conditional, future passive, inversion, reporting verbs, gerunds vs infinitives, subjunctive after *it's high time*).

## How to play

- Split the class into two teams of roughly equal ability.
- Team 1 tries to connect the **top to the bottom** of the board.
- Team 2 tries to connect the **left to the right**.
- Teams take turns choosing any hexagon. Read out the question — if the team answers correctly, they claim that hex in their colour.
- Both teams need a minimum path of 5 connected hexagons to win.
- Tap a team name above the board to switch turns manually if needed.

## Teaching tips

- Use this as a review activity after covering several grammar points — it works well at the end of a module.
- For grammar questions, ask the team to explain *why* their answer is correct before awarding the hex.
- Vocabulary answers often have synonyms or variants — accept any reasonable corporate equivalent.
- The strategic element (blocking the other team) is part of the fun — encourage teams to think about which hex helps their path most.
- If discussion gets too long on a hex, set a 30-second time limit per question.
