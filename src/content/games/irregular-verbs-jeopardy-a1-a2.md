## How it works

A 25-clue Jeopardy board for beginners practising the past simple of common irregular verbs. Teams pick a category and a value, answer the clue, and the teacher awards the points. Higher values are harder.

## Categories

- **Past simple**: complete everyday sentences (*Yesterday I ___ to work*)
- **Base form**: work backwards from *made*, *bought*, *thought*
- **Regular or irregular?**: sort verbs like *play*, *drink* and *sleep*
- **Fix the mistake**: correct errors such as *I goed* and *didn’t went*
- **Spell it!**: spell tricky forms like *bought*, *taught* and *caught*

## Classroom tips

- Project it on the board and play in two teams
- Let teams discuss for ten seconds before answering
- Follow up with the A2 board when the group is ready
