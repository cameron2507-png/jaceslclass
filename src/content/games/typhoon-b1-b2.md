## How it works

A 5×5 grid hides points and four kinds of special square. Teams answer a question to open each square, and one Typhoon can wipe out a lead.

## What’s covered

- Third and mixed conditionals
- Reported speech and the future perfect
- *I wish*, *It’s high time*, *I’d rather*, inversion with *Hardly*
- Make or do collocations
- Business English: ROI, B2B and B2C, redundancy vs dismissal, streamline, bottleneck, touch base, cut corners

## Classroom tips

- Great for end-of-course review with corporate groups
- Ask for an example sentence with each vocabulary answer
- Replay it: the special squares are shuffled every game
