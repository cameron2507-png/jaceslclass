## How it works

Each card asks for a position rather than a description, so learners need conditionals, comparatives and hedging to answer well. When the timer runs out, the group reveals the follow-up questions and challenges the speaker.

## What's included

- **48 cards**: Travel, Holidays, Food, England and General (36 cards), plus Essential oils and Biomass (12 cards)
- **Switchable industry cards**: tap Essential oils and Biomass to remove them and the deck works for any group
- **Devil's advocate mode**: the speaker has to argue the opposite of what they really think
- **Adjustable timer** of 45, 60 or 90 seconds, with 60 seconds as the default
- Cards don't repeat until the whole deck has been used

## Variations

- Use the England cards with the teacher in the hot seat
- Run a short debate: two speakers take opposite sides of the same card
- Keep the industry cards for groups who work in production or operations
