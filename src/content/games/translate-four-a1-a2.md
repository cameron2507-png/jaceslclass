A translation race built on Connect Four, made for French-speaking beginners. Each round shows a short French sentence at A1 or A2 level, and the teams race to translate it.

## How it works

- Two teams, each with their own colour
- Choose A1, A2 or both levels in Settings
- Show answer button for checking
- Undo and automatic saving
