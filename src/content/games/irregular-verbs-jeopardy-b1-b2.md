## How it works

A Jeopardy board for stronger learners who already know the common irregular verbs. It focuses on the verbs and choices that still catch out B1 and B2 students.

## Categories

- **Tricky verbs**: *lie/lay*, *rise*, *seek*, *forbid*
- **Past simple or present perfect?**: choose the right tense in context
- **Fix the mistake**: errors that even advanced learners make
- **Which verb?**: definitions that lead to an irregular verb
- **Phrasal verbs in the past**: *put off*, *came across*, *broke down*, *gave up*

## Classroom tips

- Ask teams to give a full sentence for the Which verb? answers
- Use it as a warm-up before a writing task in the past
- Works well with business English groups
